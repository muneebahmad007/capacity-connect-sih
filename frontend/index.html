How CAPACITY CONNECT Works

An intuitive step-by-step roadmap turning skills analysis into career readiness.

01

Create Profile

Sign up as a trainee, trainer, or administrator with custom domain preferences.

02

Take Assessment

Complete diagnostic subject-wise MCQ assessments to establish baseline metrics.

03

Map Competencies

Visualize strong, developing, and underperforming areas across multi-domain matrices.

04

Identify Skill Gaps

Benchmark current competency directly against industry job roles & requirements.

05

06

Learn

07
